Updates and announcements from Xenpen.

Our Return & Refund Policy

October 22, 2026

We've put our rules for returns and refunds in one easy page: how payments through Xenpen are held until you have what you paid for, what to do when something goes wrong, and how plan refunds work. Read the Return & Refund Policy; it's also in the apps under Settings.

Getting paid in the US: tax information up front

October 21, 2026

When you set up payouts in Profile → Get Paid, you now choose Personal (your Social Security number) or Business (your company's EIN). Stripe, our payment processor, keeps the number; Xenpen never sees it. Payouts wait until it's on file.

Xenpen reports sales to the IRS on Form 1099-K only when they reach the federal threshold, and you report your own income. See Help for details.

Delete notifications, messages and chats

October 20, 2026

Swipe left to delete a notification, a message or a whole chat, or select several at once, or Delete All. On your own messages, Delete for Everyone removes it for both of you. To protect members from scams, Xenpen keeps a private copy of deleted messages for one year that only moderators can see when they review a report.

Invite Friends: a sale with no commission for both of you

October 19, 2026

Share your invitation from Profile → Invite Friends. When your friend joins, names you as the person who invited them, and verifies their ID, you each get one sale with no Xenpen commission (card or bank processing fees still apply). Xenpen never messages anyone for you.

New in the apps: translate, Remove Background, widgets and more

October 19, 2026

  • Translate a listing's description or a message, right on your phone.

Listing a home now needs a plan

October 16, 2026

To list a home for sale or for rent on Xenpen, you now need a Basic plan (or Pro or Elite). Xenpen never takes a cut of a home sale or rent, so the plan is how listing homes is paid for.

Don't have a plan yet? When you post a home, we'll ask first and save it as a draft, so nothing is lost: choose a plan, then publish it from Profile → My Listings. Free members can keep posting items as before, and your plan continues until you cancel it. On Basic, you can make 3 new posts a day, like every Basic member. See Plans.

Unlimited Mondays

October 16, 2026

Every Monday, everyone on Xenpen can post as much as they like, with or without a plan, and those posts don't count toward your daily or monthly limits. It's Monday in your own time: Philippine time if you live in the Philippines, US Pacific time otherwise. Profile → Plan shows when it's on.

Also new: real estate agents, brokers, and property managers need a Basic, Pro, or Elite plan to add their real estate license and list homes. See Plans.

Xenpen moderators can now work from a computer

October 16, 2026

Xenpen's moderators can now answer Xenpen Support and review reports, licenses, address changes, TikTok level transfers, payments on hold, and account recovery requests on xenpen.com as well as in the apps, so your requests get looked at sooner.

Nothing changes about who sees what: only Xenpen moderators can open these tools, they see the same information as in the apps, and every decision is logged.

Two-step verification and App Lock

October 15, 2026

You can now add extra protection to your Xenpen account, on the iPhone and Android apps and on xenpen.com:

  • Two-step verification: go to Profile → Settings → Security → Turn On and scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, and others). Signing in then asks for the 6-digit code from the app.
  • Recovery codes: you get 10 one-time codes to keep somewhere safe, in case you lose your phone.
  • App Lock: in the apps, have Xenpen ask for Face ID, Touch ID, or your fingerprint each time you open it. Your face or fingerprint never leaves your phone.

See Help for step-by-step instructions.
